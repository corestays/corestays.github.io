EV Charging

Tesla Universal Wall Connector · works with Tesla and most other EVs

Diagrams: Tesla

1Using the built-in J1772 adapter

Remove

  1. With the handle in its dock, press the black button on the adapter (where the handle meets the dock) and pull. The handle comes out with the J1772 adapter attached.
  2. Plug the adapter into your car's charge port.

When you're done

  1. Unlock your car (the plug won't release while the car is locked).
  2. Press and hold the button on the handle and pull the plug out of the car.

Dock

  1. Wrap the cable counter-clockwise around the Wall Connector.
  2. Push the handle, with the adapter still attached, back into the dock so the next guest can use either plug.

2Starting a charge in a non-Tesla car

  1. Get the Tesla app (App Store or Google Play) and sign in or create a free account.
  2. In the app, open Charge Your Other EV and select this charger on the map.
    Don't see it? Tap the filter icon, select both lightning-bolt icons, check Private Destination Chargers, and apply.
  3. Tap Get Started, add a payment method, and tap Save. (You need Wi-Fi or cell signal.)
  4. Plug in using the adapter (Part 1).
  5. Scan the QR code on the left side of the Wall Connector's base. Charging starts automatically and a green light shows on the base.
  6. Check charge status and cost in the app. Past sessions are in your charge history.
Not charging? Make sure the plug is fully seated, the car isn't scheduled to charge later, and (non-Tesla cars) the session started in the app. Tesla's troubleshooting link is below.
